How to use the DSY Training site

Training home

🧭 New Team Leader? Start here

Learn it in this order. Each step has a short training module (🎬) and the matching part of the Team Leader guide, which follows the same order.

  1. See what your volunteers see. Getting started (signing in, the home page, booking a training night) and Part 1 of the roster guide (booking a shift).
  2. Your dashboard. 🎬 Module 1 · guide sections 1 to 4 (signing in, the dashboard, tasks, Ask DSY).
  3. People and passwords. 🎬 Module 2 · guide section 5 (the People page: adding people, teams, VTLs, password resets).
  4. Rosters. Part 2 of the roster guide (manager controls) · guide sections 6 and 7 (rosters and roster emails).
  5. Crewing gaps. 🎬 Module 3 · guide section 8.
  6. Training nights. 🎬 Module 4 · guide section 9, then Running a session and the MDT simulator guide.
  7. Relief cover. 🎬 Module 5 · guide section 10.
  8. Your team page. 🎬 Module 6 · guide section 11.
  9. When you need them. Guide sections 12 to 15: volunteer contribution, dispatch, setting up an AR course and the retro car dash.

🧭 New Volunteer Team Leader? Start here

As a VTL you run your team's training and manage its roster. Learn it in this order:

  1. See what your crew sees. Getting started and Part 1 of the roster guide.
  2. Your team dashboard. Open your Team Page and tap Team dashboard: today's roster, recent dispatches, your team's contribution and training.
  3. Managing the roster. Part 2 of the roster guide (manager controls).
  4. Running training. Running a session, then the MDT simulator guide.
