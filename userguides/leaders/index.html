1. Signing in and finding your pages

Every DSY page uses one sign-in: your ambulance ID and your password (the sign-in page also accepts an email). The same account works in the training app.

  1. Open dsytraining.com. Signed in as a leader, you see Dashboard and Grant access links on the home page.
  2. The dashboard is at /dashboard/ (phone and tablet) and /dashboard/wide/ (computer screen). Each links to the other at the bottom.
  3. Leader pages are linked along the bottom of the dashboard: Crewing gaps, Relief cover, Roster emails, Grant access and the Retro car dash.
Tip: add the dashboard to your phone's Home Screen. In Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

A page you're not allowed to see says "Facilitators only" or sends you to sign in. If you've been signed out, sign in again and the page you wanted opens.

Your cluster

Each Team Leader looks after one cluster: Lower Eyre, Central Eyre or West Coast. Pages open on your own cluster, and most have an All clusters chip to look wider. You can view other clusters' training but you can't change it. The lead instructor has no cluster set, which means all clusters, and can change everything.

2. The dashboard

The dashboard brings the day together on one screen. It refreshes itself every 10 minutes; tap ↻ Refresh to refresh now.

Roster todayEvery team's roster for today, one row per team, one square per hour: green = 2 or more crew, yellow = 1, red = none. This hour is outlined. Use the cluster chips to filter. Tap a square to see who is on at that hour (name, level, flexibility and finish time); Back to now returns to the current hour. Team names open that team's roster. Operations overview → opens the full operations screen.

Training sessionsThe next session, a 4-week calendar for your cluster, and a count of sessions in the next 4 weeks that have no lesson yet (tap it to fix them in the training app).

Buttons along the top

  • DSY home, Training app.
  • 📅 My Google Calendar opens your calendar. Tap ⚙︎ beside it to paste your own calendar link (for example a shared calendar). It is saved to your DSY account, so it works on any device, and only you see it. Leave it empty for your normal Google Calendar.
3. Your task list (and Siri)

Each Team Leader has their own task list: other leaders can't see yours. (A relief Team Leader covering for you does see your tasks while the cover lasts: section 8.)

  1. Type the task in Add a task, pick a due date if you want one, and tap Add.
  2. Tick the box when it's done.
  3. Tap the words to change them. Tap × to delete.

Tasks that are overdue show a red date; tasks due soon show an amber date. Show done lists the last 20 finished tasks.

Siri quick add

The lead instructor can add a task by voice: "Hey Siri, DSY task", then say the task. It uses an iPhone Shortcut with a private key that adds only to that person's list. Any other leader who wants this needs their own key: ask the lead instructor to set one up.

4. Ask DSY

Ask DSY on the dashboard answers "how do I…?" questions about the DSY site, for Team Leaders only. For example:

  • "How do I reset a volunteer's password?"
  • "Why can't I edit a West Coast session?"
  • "How do I hand over to a relief TL while I'm on leave?"

It answers only from this guide and the roster guide, and links to the part it used. If the guides don't cover something, it says so. Ask the lead instructor then.

Not for clinical questions. Ask DSY doesn't answer clinical or protocol questions: use the SAAS clinical site and app. Don't type patient details or other private information into it.
  • Answers are written by AI (Claude). Check anything important on the page itself.
  • It remembers the last few questions while the page is open, so you can ask a follow-up. Start again clears it.
  • Each leader can ask up to 40 questions a day. Questions and answers are kept so the lead instructor can improve the guide.
5. Crewing gaps

/dashboard/coverage/ (Crewing gaps at the bottom of the dashboard) finds the hours in the next 8 days that don't meet your own rules. It only finds the gaps: you decide what to do about each one (paramedic cover or other support).

Setting your rules

  1. Tap + Add a group. A group is one or more teams checked together (for example two neighbouring towns). You can choose from your own cluster's teams.
  2. Set the minimum crew across the group (1 to 8).
  3. For each level (PARA, AO, AR, AA, REC) choose Counts, Only with a counted person in the same town, or Doesn't count.
  4. Choose the shortest gap to list (any, or 1 to 12 hours).
  5. Tap Save rules. Your rules are yours only; a relief Team Leader covering for you can read them.

A new group starts with: minimum 2; PARA, AO and AR count; AA only with one of those in the same town; REC doesn't count; gaps of 6 hours or more.

Reading the results

  • For each group: the number of gaps and hours, a bar for each day, and each gap with who was booked.
  • Gaps are counted by volunteer shift: Day 08:00–18:00 and Night 18:00–08:00. A gap that runs across several shifts is listed once for each shift it touches, marked "Part of a longer gap".
  • For groups of two or more teams, it also lists towns that have nobody counted in their own right.
  • Gaps that have already finished are left out. Today goes back to today; Copy as text copies the list to paste into a message or email.
6. Volunteer contribution

/dashboard/contribution/ shows, for each person: rostered hours (shifts already worked, not future ones), the number of shifts, jobs paged to their team while they were rostered on, and P1 jobs. Tap a person to see their job list.

  • Choose the period: all, this month, last month, the last 30 or 7 days.
  • Use the Cluster chips, then the Team chips, to narrow it down.
  • Paramedic cover and placeholders (like "LEP40") are hidden unless you tick them.
What it can't tell you: the pager feed doesn't say who actually turned out, so a job counts for everyone rostered on at the time. Jobs later cancelled or stood down are included. Roster history starts in early November 2026 (later for Central Eyre and West Coast).
7. Dispatch

The dashboard's Dispatch · last 7 days panel shows jobs paged across the Eyre Peninsula. Full dispatch screen → opens /dispatch/.

  • Jobs run by Port Lincoln (PL) and Ceduna (CD) crews are hidden when the page opens. A button in the panel shows or hides them.
  • Jobs on the team leader call sign (CO, where the number is the person) are shown as "Team leader", not as a team.
  • The job list is copied into DSY from the pager feed by a scheduled run. If the newest job looks old, tell the lead instructor.
8. Relief cover

Going on leave? /dashboard/relief/ (Relief cover at the bottom of the dashboard) hands your leader access to a relief Team Leader until an end date.

  1. Under Hand over, choose the Relief Team Leader.
  2. Set From and Until.
  3. Tap Start cover and confirm.
They get, until the end dateYour dashboards and your task list; training for your cluster, with editing; volunteer contribution and dispatch; Grant access and password resets.
They don't getYour Google Calendar link; resetting a leader's password; making anyone a leader; handing the cover on to someone else.

The cover ends by itself on the end date. You, the relief Team Leader or the lead instructor can end it early with End now (the relief Team Leader sees End my cover). The relief Team Leader may need to refresh an open page when it starts or ends. While covering, the dashboard shows a banner and your tasks are labelled with your name.

9. Training sessions and lessons

Training is run in the training app, /training/ (Training app on the dashboard). As a leader you see the tabs Calendar, Sessions, Lessons and People.

Your cluster

The app opens on your own cluster. The Cluster chips on Calendar and People show the others, but another cluster's sessions are view only (a notice says whose they are) and their team boxes are locked. Lessons are one shared library for everyone.

Setting up your training nights

  1. On Calendar, tap Set up training nights.
  2. Choose the team, then who the nights are for: Team Leader visits (leader-led, with you as facilitator) or Team training (team-led; choose the facilitator later by opening each night).
  3. Pick the first night (the nights repeat on that weekday), how often (every week, fortnightly, every third or fourth week), the last date (it starts at the end of that school year) and the times.
  4. SA school holidays and public holidays are left out unless you untick them. The list shows every date, with the ones left out crossed through and the reason. Nights the team already has are kept as they are.
  5. Tap Create sessions.

To do your visits and the team's training on alternate weeks, set up your visits first, then set up team training starting one week later. Add an extra night with Add one session; to remove a night, open it and tap Delete session.

Sessions and courses

  • Open a session to choose its lessons (a session can have several, in order), see bookings, take Attendance and write a Reflection (your notes on how it went; members never see them).
  • Add a course (under Teams and courses) makes a separate set of sessions, for example for recruits from several teams: pick the first date, the last date and the days between sessions. A new course belongs to your cluster.
  • Delete session removes its bookings and attendance too, and can't be undone.
The dashboard counts sessions in the next 4 weeks that have no lesson. Tap the count to go straight to them.

Lessons

The Lessons tab is the library: New lesson adds one (title, summary, resources and links, files), and Use this lesson in adds it to a session. The dashboard's Lessons tile opens this tab directly (/training/#lessons).

People: putting someone in a team or course

  1. Open the People tab and tap the person's name.
  2. Under Teams and courses, tick each team or course they belong to. Untick to take them off. Each tick saves straight away.

They then see that team's sessions and appear on its Team list and attendance. You can only tick teams in your own cluster; other clusters' teams are greyed out. Someone who isn't in the People list yet has no account: add their ambulance ID on /access/ first and ask them to sign up (section 10).

Can't see a team's members? The People list and Team lists only show people who have signed up. Crews loaded in advance with their team (for example the Central Eyre teams) are already approved: when each person signs up with their ambulance ID, DSY puts them into their team by itself, with nothing to tick. Until then the team shows only its leader. Ask the crew to sign up at dsytraining.com/signin.

The person's page also has Reset password (section 10). Only the lead instructor can change someone's cluster or make someone a leader.

Team pages: banner photo and photo slides

Each team has a Team Page (/teams/) that members see when they sign in: a banner photo behind the team name, the Training schedule and Learn on your own buttons, photo slides to swipe through, About the team, and links.

  1. Open your team's page and tap Edit this page (leaders only). Or open the Region page (/region/) and tap Edit page on the team's tile.
  2. Banner photo: choose a photo to put it behind the team name. It replaces the town photo DSY put there. If the wrong part shows (a head cut off), drag the Move the photo slider until the preview looks right; it saves when you let go. Remove banner photo takes it off.
  3. Photo slides: choose one or more photos to add them. Each one can have a caption; Move up changes the order and Remove takes one out.
  4. About this team, the roster link and the links list are in the same form: tap Save team page for those. Photos save as soon as they upload.
Photos must be JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB each. Only use photos you took or have permission to use, and check that crews and patients are happy to be in them.
10. Grant access and passwords

Only people whose ambulance ID is on the approved list can make a DSY account. /access/ (Grant access on the home page and at the bottom of the dashboard) is where leaders manage that list.

Adding people

  1. Under Add IDs, paste ambulance IDs, one per line or separated by commas. You can add a name after the ID: 12345, Sam Taylor. Or choose a CSV file (ID in the first column, name in the second).
  2. Choose Add as Student or Facilitator, and the Team they belong to (or No team).
  3. Tap Add to the list. They can now sign up on the sign-in page with that ID. Sign-up asks only for the ID and a password: the account takes the name you entered here, so add names with the IDs (anyone added without one is asked for their name once, straight after signing up).

Someone who has already signed up joins the team straight away. Someone who hasn't signed up yet shows under Not signed up yet and joins the team automatically when they sign up (section 9).

The list

Filter by Everyone, Not signed up yet, Facilitators or Switched off. Remove switches the account off (they can no longer sign in); add them back to switch it on again.

The Team column shows each person's teams. For someone signed up, Add to a team puts them in another team (take people off a team in the training app, People tab). For someone not signed up yet, change the team in the box. You can only choose teams in your own cluster.

Password resets

Volunteers can't reset their own password (there's no email on file), so a leader does it:

  1. Use Reset password on /access/, or on the person's page in the training app (People tab).
  2. Type a temporary password (8 or more characters) and tell the person.
  3. They sign in with it and are asked to choose their own straight away.
Never post a password in a group chat or team messages. Only the lead instructor can reset another leader's password.
11. Rosters for Team Leaders

Each team has its own roster page. Everything crews and roster managers do is in the Crew Shift Roster guide. What matters for Team Leaders:

  • The rosters are moving from the Google Sheets (Apps Scripts) to DSY's own database. All three clusters move together on one switch day. Until then each team's roster works as it does now. The new version is being tried on the Summer Bay test roster: see Part 3 of the roster guide.
  • On the new roster, Team Leaders count as roster managers for their cluster's teams: you can book someone else, add a placeholder such as "LEP40" (Not on the list…), send the roster to Country Rostering and set up its weekly schedule. There's no manager password: your sign-in is enough.
  • The dashboard's Roster today, On shift and Crewing gaps all read the team rosters, so a booking shows there within a few minutes.
12. Roster email settings

/dashboard/roster-settings/ (Roster emails at the bottom of the dashboard) holds the addresses the new roster emails go to. It applies to rosters on the new system only (the Summer Bay test roster for now).

  • Addresses per cluster: trial address, area manager, Country Rostering and an optional CC. Tap Save addresses.
  • Team managers: add or remove each team's roster managers and the email their alerts go to (crews never see it).
  • Send a test email checks the emails arrive.
  • Live from date (lead instructor only): until a cluster's date is set it is a trial. Every email then goes only to the trial address, marked "TRIAL", and never to Country Rostering.
  • Recent roster emails lists the last 15 with any error.

Roster emails come from "<Team> Team Roster" (for example "Tumby Bay Team Roster"), and replies go to that team's roster managers.

13. The retro car dash

/dashboard/car/ is just for fun: the same live numbers as an old car's instrument panel. Fuel = the share of today's team-hours still to come that have crew; speedometer = crew on shift now; tacho = jobs in the last 24 hours; temperature = today's top for your town (tap to change). Warning lights show low crew, a P1 in 24 hours, an overdue task, lessons needed, a hot and windy day coming, or a source that didn't load. Tap a gauge to open its page.

14. If something isn't working
  1. Check your reception, then tap ↻ Refresh or reload the page.
  2. Signed out or "Facilitators only"? Sign in again at /signin/ with your ambulance ID.
  3. A panel says it couldn't load: that source (a team roster, the weather or the pager feed) didn't answer. The rest of the page still works. Try again later.
  4. You can't change a session or team: it belongs to another cluster (view only).
  5. Still stuck? Ask DSY, or tell the lead instructor what you were doing and what you saw.
