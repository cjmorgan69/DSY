Part 1 Crew members

1. Getting started

Open your team's roster

Use the roster link your manager sent you. Each team has its own link, and the team name shows in the badge at the top of the page.

Tip: add the roster to your phone's home screen so it opens like an app. In Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. In Chrome, tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
Roster managers: the home-screen version can stop the Country Rostering PDF view from opening, so use your browser for manager tasks (see section 10).

Enter your name and clinical level

  1. Type your Name at the top of the page.
  2. Choose your Clinical level: PARA, AO, AR, AA or REC (recruit / observer).

The roster remembers both on this phone or computer, so you only need to enter them once.

Always spell your name the same way. My bookings finds your shifts by name, so a nickname, different spelling or extra space will hide them.
2. When the roster opens

The roster is fetched fresh every time it opens. This can take a few seconds, longer with poor reception.

You may see first

Showing the roster saved on this device at 7:42 am · updating… Booking opens once it's up to date.

This is the roster as it was the last time you opened it on this device, so you can look straight away. A quote or tip may also show while you wait. Booking and cancelling unlock once the fresh roster arrives.

Ready

12 active bookings loaded for Summer Bay.
The saved copy only works in a normal browser tab. Private or incognito tabs forget it (and your name) each time they close.
3. Booking a shift
  1. Choose the Start date.
  2. Choose a Start time.
  3. Choose a Finish time. Shifts can be 1 to 24 hours and can finish the next day.
  4. If you have a Flexibility restriction, choose A, B or C. Otherwise leave N — No restriction.
  5. Check the summary below the form.
  6. Tap Confirm shift.

You'll see "Your shift was booked successfully." Afterwards the date clears but the times stay selected, so booking the same hours on another day just needs a new date.

The roster checks that

  • The shift doesn't start in the past.
  • You don't already have a shift at the same time.
  • No more than three people are booked in any hour.
  • Only one recruit (REC) is booked in any hour.
A recruit takes one of the three places but is an observer, so they don't count towards crew coverage.
4. Reading the roster

Timeline

Roster days run from 08:00 until 07:59 the next morning. A shift at 03:00 Tuesday shows under Monday.

Each hour is coloured by how many crew are on (observers not counted):

Red
No crew booked.
Yellow
One crew member booked.
Green
Two or more crew booked.

Tap a day to open or close it, or use Expand all and Collapse all.

Booking list

The Booking list tab shows every active booking in a table. Search by name, level, flexibility or date. The Op column is the crew's operational code, worked out automatically from who is booked.

14-day team roster

📋 View Team Roster — Next 14 Days opens a printable table in a new window. You can pick other dates, move to the next 14 days, or print it.

5. Changing or cancelling a shift

Tap My bookings to see your upcoming shifts. Each one has an Edit and a Cancel button until the shift finishes.

Changing a shift

  1. Tap Edit.
  2. The panel opens with your current start, finish and flexibility already filled in. Change only what you need, such as an earlier finish.
  3. Check the line that shows Was … → now …. Any problem (for example, an hour that's already full) shows in red.
  4. Tap Save changes, or Keep as is to close.
  • Save changes stays greyed out until something has changed.
  • If you move the start, the finish time stays where it was where possible.
  • Once a shift has started, only the finish and flexibility can change.

Cancelling a shift

  1. Tap Cancel in My bookings or the Booking list.
  2. Confirm the message that appears.
  3. The shift greys out and shows Cancelling… while the roster saves it, then disappears with "Shift cancelled."
Poor reception? Wait. The roster tries up to three times and checks that the change went through, so there's no need to tap again.

If the roster can't get any reply at all, you'll see:

Couldn't confirm the cancellation: no reply from the roster. It may still have gone through. Refresh the roster when you have reception before trying again.

When you have reception, tap Refresh roster and check My bookings before trying again.

6. Changes to a roster already sent

Your roster manager sends a printed roster to Country Rostering each week. The Roster Submission Status box shows how far it has been sent:

Roster Submission Status

ROSTER SENT for Summer Bay up until Mon 19 Oct 2026, 08:00. Changes affecting shifts before this cut-off require notification to Country Rostering.

You can still book, change and cancel shifts before the cut-off. Country Rostering just needs to know.

What happens

  • Shift more than 24 hours away: Country Rostering is emailed automatically. You'll see "Country Rostering has been notified by email."
  • Shift within 24 hours, or the email couldn't be sent: a message asks you to phone Country Rostering. Please do it straight away.

Country Rostering:

1300 850 985

Your roster managers are also emailed about every change inside the sent roster.

7. Team messages

Team messages is a shared notice board for your team: availability, leave, and other roster notes.

Posting a message

  1. Check your name is entered at the top of the page.
  2. Open Team messages and type your note (up to 2,000 characters).
  3. Tap Post message.

Deleting a message

Messages you posted from this phone or computer show a Delete button. On another device, or after clearing your browser data, ask your roster manager to remove it.

Never post confidential information. No patient or clinical details, and no private contact or personal information. Everyone on the team roster can read the board.
8. If something isn't working
  1. Check you have internet or mobile reception.
  2. Tap Refresh roster.
  3. Check your name and clinical level are entered correctly.
  4. Reload the page in your browser.
  5. If it still isn't working, contact your roster manager.

Common questions

  • My bookings is empty. Check your name is spelt exactly as when you booked.
  • Buttons are greyed out. The roster is still loading or saving. Wait a moment, or refresh.
  • "Unable to verify hard-copy status." Refresh the roster before changing bookings.
  • Another device looks different. Your name and saved roster are kept separately on each device and browser.

Part 2 Roster managers

9. Manager controls and password

At the bottom of the Book a shift card, tap Manager controls to open them. They contain:

  • Open Country Rostering PDF view — 7 days for printing and marking the roster as sent.
  • Edit sent cut-off and Reset sent status, for corrections only.

Every manager action asks for the roster manager password for your team. The password isn't saved, so it's asked for each time.

Keep the password to roster managers. Don't post it in Team messages or share it with crew.
10. Printing the 7-day roster for Country Rostering
A computer gives the most reliable printout. A phone works too; see the phone tips below.
Use your browser, not the home-screen icon. If you added the roster to your home screen, open the roster link in Safari, Chrome or Edge instead for manager tasks. The home-screen version can stop the PDF view and its Mark as sent panel from opening.

Open the PDF view

  1. Tap Refresh roster so the roster is up to date.
  2. Open Manager controls.
  3. Tap Open Country Rostering PDF view — 7 days. It opens in a new window or tab.
  4. Set First roster day (7 days shown) and tap Show roster, or use Next 7 days.
  5. Check the dates and bookings shown are right.

Print or save as PDF

Tap 🖨️ Print / Save PDF and use these settings:

Printer / destinationSave as PDF
Paper sizeA4
LayoutLandscape
Scale100%. The roster already shrinks itself to fit.
Background graphicsOn, so the Op colours print.

The roster prints on two pages: 08:00–19:59 and 20:00–07:59.

Phone tips

  • Check the print preview shows two landscape pages with no columns cut off.
  • Some phone browsers (for example Microsoft Edge) size the page differently. If the preview doesn't fit, lower the scale in the print options until it does.
  • If you see "Please allow pop-ups to view the Country Rostering roster.", allow pop-ups for the roster in your browser settings and try again.

Send the PDF to Country Rostering in the usual way, then mark it as sent (next section).

11. Marking the roster as sent

Once the PDF has gone to Country Rostering, record it from the same PDF view. This sets the hard-copy cut-off to the end of the 7 days you printed.

  1. With the printed 7 days still showing, tap Mark as sent up until …. The button shows the cut-off date and time.
  2. A panel opens below the buttons. Read the question, enter the Roster manager password, and tap Confirm and mark as sent.
  3. You'll see "Marked as sent up until …". Close the PDF view and refresh the main roster. The Roster Submission Status box now shows the new cut-off.

Where the cut-off falls

The cut-off is 08:00 on the morning after the last roster day printed. For example, printing Monday 12 to Sunday 18 October sets the cut-off at Monday 19 October, 08:00:

  • Changes to shifts starting before 08:00 Monday 19 need Country Rostering notified.
  • A shift starting at 08:00 Monday 19 or later is outside the sent roster.
If the button reads Already sent up to here, those days are already covered. Choose a later first roster day if you're sending the next week.
12. After it's sent: changes and emails

When anyone books, changes or cancels a shift that starts before the cut-off:

  • More than 24 hours before the shift: Country Rostering is emailed automatically, with the crew member, times and change. For an edited shift, the email includes the previous times.
  • Within 24 hours: no email to Country Rostering. The crew member is asked to phone 1300 850 985.
  • Always: the team's roster manager and the area manager (where their emails are set up) get a [Roster Alert] email saying what changed and whether Country Rostering was emailed.
If an alert says the Country Rostering email failed, or the crew member was asked to phone, check that Country Rostering has been told.

Change Log

Every booking, change, cancellation and cut-off update is recorded on the Change Log tab of the roster's Google Sheet: when, which team, what happened and who did it. Use it to check what happened to a shift.

13. Correcting the sent cut-off

Normally you only need Mark as sent. Use these from Manager controls only to fix a mistake.

Edit sent cut-off

  1. Tap Edit sent cut-off.
  2. Choose the date and time the roster was sent up until.
  3. Tap Save sent cut-off and enter the password.

The cut-off can only move later. To move it earlier, reset it first, then set it again.

Reset sent status

Reset sent status clears the cut-off for your team only. Changes then stop triggering Country Rostering notices until the roster is marked as sent again. It doesn't change any bookings.

Only reset if the sent status genuinely no longer applies. While it's reset, Country Rostering isn't told about changes.
14. Removing team messages

Roster managers can remove any message in Team messages:

  1. Open Team messages.
  2. Tap Manager delete on the message.
  3. Confirm, then enter the roster manager password.

Remove anything with confidential or personal information as soon as you see it.
