Volunteer Team Leaders

One per team. A Volunteer Team Leader (VTL) gets facilitator access, can manage their own team's roster, and has a team dashboard (roster, dispatches, volunteer contribution, training and weather for their team). They are not a Team Leader: no Grant access and no other teams. Pick from the people signed up in that team.
