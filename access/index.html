Relief cover. You can add people, put them in teams and reset passwords. Changing roles, taking people off a team and switching accounts off stay with the Team Leaders.

Add people

One ambulance ID per line, or separated by commas. Add a name after the ID if you like: 12345, Sam Taylor. A CSV file works too: ID in the first column, name in the second. Choose a Team to put them in it: someone already signed up joins straight away, anyone else joins when they sign up.

Volunteer Team Leaders

One per team. A Volunteer Team Leader (VTL) gets facilitator access, can manage their own team's roster, and has a team dashboard (roster, dispatches, volunteer contribution, training and weather for their team). They are not a Team Leader: no People page and no other teams. Pick from the people signed up in that team.

The page opens on your own cluster; people in other clusters are view only. Take someone off a team with the ✕ on the team. Remove switches the whole account off: they cannot sign in again, and a session already open ends within about an hour. Add them back to restore it. Leaders cannot be switched off here. Reset password gives someone a temporary one; they choose their own when they next sign in.
