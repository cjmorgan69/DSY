Ambulance Responder course: everything for facilitators
Run the course
1. Build the courseCreate the course group, then pick the days, dates, start times and locations (Build the course). 2. StudentsAdd students from the people list. They book their dates from their training calendar. 3. InductionTick off each student's pre-course induction and declaration. Vendor and Ambulance Cover forms. 4. Dates and rollOpen a date to see who's booked and mark the roll. 5. Skill sign-offPick the course (or stations) and open each skill's sign-off in saaslearn, filtered to the station. 6. ProgressWho has done which days, and a printable attendance record.

All of these are in Workshop bookings except skill sign-off. Step-by-step help: Setting up an AR course in the Team Leader Guide.

Course days and material

Each day follows the course completion checklist, plus Operational Safety (Day 1), DRiVE Principles and Knowledge (Day 3), consolidation (Days 8 and 9) and the Stress Management webinar (Day 10). Material that exists opens from here; grey tiles are placeholders for material still to build.

InductionBefore the course, as soon as the student has a SAAS ID

Induction

ID card and key request (photo), pager, uniform, AR course enrolment, vendor form and Ambulance Cover (welcome email), multi-factor authentication, SAAS and eLearning logins, the infection control prac, Fatigue Management, and the induction declaration.
