## Anaphylaxis

Anaphylaxis skills stations (`/stations/anaphylaxis/`, 27 Oct 2026): a practical night the team runs without the
instructor: 4 stations (recognise and call; EpiPen + glass ampoule draw-up and IM into a pad; oxygen NRB; moving the
patient) then the student walk-through together; partner tick lists saved on the phone, Share the record to the
instructor; instructor setup sheet; #print gives an A4 pack with QR codes to the live pages. Sources: CPP-003-AO,
CPPRO-008/010/011 (review June 2020 passed), CPPRO-001 (hypotension = no mobility assessment), SAAS Clinical Skills
Study Guide v3.2 Oct 2026 Oxygen (owner's PDF). Owner: floor or hard to extricate but BP OK after adrenaline = call
the EOC Clinician; sitting patient self-transfers (bum shuffle) to the carry chair; NRB always 15 L/min; adrenaline
is a glass ampoule; EpiPen trainer not always available. Patient cards, EOC replies and manual task practice are
DSY-written (owner to review). Linked from Run a session via `stations` in `hub/topics.js`. Live 27 Oct 2026.

## Falls

Falls skills stations (`/stations/falls/`, 27 Oct 2026, same engine): 1 on the floor (obs, BGL with a consenting
partner or the control solution, head to toe, log roll, CPP-045-AO transport list), 2 CPPRO-001 mobility check from
the floor and the chair and the lift plan, 3 the Raizer II lifting chair (owner asked; from the maker's Raizer II
User Manual, Liftup / DHG Issue 1 Jan 2023, because SAAS has no Raizer procedure and the study guide has no page:
contraindications injury from the fall or can't rule one out, not fully conscious, over 150 kg; backrests before
legs; reassess sitting per CPPRO-001), 4 injuries (cardboard splint, arm sling, collar and cuff, elevation sling;
hip: transport, no traction for NOF per CPPRO-028), 5 staying home (FROP, Braden, CRU call, EOC consult). No falls
walk-through exists, so the finish is two DSY-written jobs (Dorothy 83 long lie + wrist, transport; Bill 76, stays
home, FROP 1). Sources: CSSG v3.2 BGL, head to toe, log roll, slings, cardboard splint; CPP-045-AO, CTL-003,
CTL-010, CPPRO-001/002/003. Patient cards and jobs are DSY-written (owner to review). Live 27 Oct 2026, lesson added
(the owner confirmed crews carry the Raizer II and SAAS has no Raizer instructions).

## Chest pain and ACPO

first from a list of suggested stations for every topic without them): 1 aspirin and GTN (CPPRO-008 three-question
check, CPPRO-009 aspirin chewed and GTN tablet or spray, limits side by side for CPP-007-AO and CPP-001-AO, own-GTN

ECG and SpO2 (CSSG p42 to 49: lead colours, monitor mode, D-ECG with age and sex only, artefact, Event key; 4-lead

pain SpO2 above 94% else nasal cannula, ACPO high flow NRB), 4 listening to the chest (CSSG p50, AO only), ACPO
posture, no walking with active chest pain (CPPRO-001), carry chair and stretcher head up (DSY). Finish: Graham or
Kevin walk-through. Linked from both hub topics. Live 27 Oct 2026, lesson added.

from the SAAS General Operation Cert IV task card (owner's photo, 12-lead crossed to 4-lead): default screen, Jog dial
leads, 4-lead print, NIBP Auto interval, views, shortcut keys, alarm autolimits, screenshot, trend and mission summary.
Card items left out: module separate/reassemble, CPR screen, charge and defib in demo mode, LT ECG, CO2 (crossed out).
On a branch, not merged.

## Asthma

Asthma skills stations (`/stations/asthma/`, 27 Oct 2026): 1 anaphylaxis first and severity by speech and distress,
not SpO2 (CPP-050-AO, CTL-071), 2 salbutamol MDI and spacer (CPPRO-012, 12 or 6 puffs, mask on the spacer for small
children; 6-year-old = consult), 3 the nebuliser (salbutamol 5 mg + ipratropium 500 microg, or half of each + 3 mL
saline to 5 mL for 1 to 6; under 10 mL, upright, 6 to 8 L/min, extraction), 4 life threatening: high flow and the
BVM on a manikin (CSSG p15 to 18; slow and gentle 4/min per CTL-071). No training inhalers (owner): expired live
salbutamol MDIs labelled TRAINING (CPPRO-008 allows expired non-controlled medicines as isolated, labelled training
stock); test puffs into the air, spacer puffs only onto a manikin, the partner breathes through a spacer with no
inhaler; nebs with saline only; stations have manikins (owner). Nobody breathes in a real medicine (DSY rule).
Finish: Karen walk-through. Live 27 Oct 2026, lesson added.

## Stroke

Stroke skills stations (`/stations/stroke/`, 27 Oct 2026): 1 BGL first then ROSIER and ACT (CTL-066) on a partner, 2
the clock and the Code Stroke call (CPP-016-AO v6.0 criteria, last seen well, one-minute ISBAR; local notification

Frank walk-through.

## Hypoglycaemia, and the GCS station shared with stroke

Hypoglycaemia skills stations (`/stations/hypoglycaemia/`, 27 Oct 2026): 1 BGL, calibration, quality control (LO 1.7
to 3.4, HI 12.6 to 20.9) and error codes (CSSG p19 to 23), 2 oral glucose up to 15 g (CPP-009-AO, CPPRO-009; under 2
= consult), 3 glucagon on EOC Clinician advice: repeat back, dose table, mix by CPPRO-010's general dry-powder steps
(check against the station's kit), IM into a pad (CPPRO-011), 4 the drowsy patient's airway (same airway block as
stroke). Finish: Ron walk-through. Both nights have station 5 "GCS: score it right" (owner asked: GCS is hard for
AOs to score accurately): CTL-075 GCS (Glasgow Coma Score) adult, 2 to 4 years and baby tables as printed (adult
motor 5 = purposeful movement; the baby scale is headed "≥ 1 year old" in CTL-075, flagged as probably meaning under
1, and there is no 1 to 2 year scale), E V M recorded separately, RDR GCS zones (CTL-070), seven DSY-written cards;
the SAAS Clinical app has a GCS calculator. Both live 27 Oct 2026, lessons added.
