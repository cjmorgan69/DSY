1. Finding your topic
  1. On the DSY home page, tap Run a session (or open /hub/run/). It's for facilitators: you need a facilitator or leader sign-in.
  2. Tap a category, for example Cardiac and respiratory, then the topic (for example Chest pain). Use your phone's back button to step back.
  3. The topic shows the same tiles every time, in two rows:
    • On the night: Presentation, Scenario, MDT, PCR.
    • Before and after: Stations, Microlearning, Customise, Feedback QR.

A faint tile marked "Not made yet" means that part doesn't exist for this topic yet. Topics still being built show as "Coming soon". Some topics also have a fold-out Glossary; meanings marked as DSY-written should be checked before the night.

2. Before the night
  1. Send the student link. Tap Student link (Copy or share) and paste it into your team's group chat. It points to the topic's microlearning module, so crews can do the video, key points and quiz first.
  2. Read the facilitator version. Open the Scenario tile and read it through, including the facilitator prompts.
  3. Plan the night in the training app. Check the session in Training sessions has its lesson and place. Crews book a spot there.
  4. Gear: if you're running stations, check their equipment list (section 6). For a case night you need a screen or TV.
3. Running the walk-through scenario

The Scenario tile opens the walk-through: a whole job from the page to handover, told in steps.

  • Facilitators see a Facilitator / Student switch. Facilitator shows the expected answers and your prompts; Student shows what the crew sees. Students only ever get the student version.
  • Break points stop the story with a question for the crew. Ask it, let them talk, then read the answer from your version.
  • Decision tiles ask the crew to choose. Let the room vote before you tap to show the answer.
  • Flash cards recap the key points.
Run it with the crew around a table or a screen. Give each person a role (attending, driving, radio) and swap at each break point.
4. Running a case night with the slides

Some topics have a Presentation tile: a whole case night as one slide show (intro video, key points, the job's break points and decision tiles, MDT screenshots, the PCR, and a debrief). It takes about two hours.

  • Use the arrow keys or a clicker to move through. Questions appear one per click; decision tiles let the room vote, then a click shows the answer.
  • Notes shows the expected answers for the slide. Presenter view opens a second window with the notes, the next slide and a timer, for your laptop while the room watches the TV.
  • Full screen for the TV. Guide explains the night's running order.
  • The last slide is a feedback QR code for the room to scan.
5. The MDT and PCR on the night
  • MDT (Load the job on the iPad) opens the MDT simulator with this topic's job ready to send. The crew works the data terminal from dispatch to clear. How to run it is in the MDT simulator guide.
  • PCR (Write the patient report card) opens the PCR trainer with the same patient, so the crew writes the patient care record after the walk-through.
6. Skills stations

The Stations tile opens a practical night the team can run without an instructor: a few hands-on stations, then the walk-through together.

  • Start with Before you start: the equipment list and how the stations run.
  • Partners tick off each skill on the phone. When finished, the crew can share their record with the instructor.
  • The instructor setup sheet lists what to lay out. The print view gives an A4 pack with QR codes to the live pages, for a station that has no phone handy.
7. Using your own town and hospital

The Customise tile changes a walk-through's local details: the patient's name and age, town, station, hospital, travel times, time of the page, season and crew names.

  1. Tap Customise and fill in the details you want to change. Leave the rest as they are.
  2. Open the facilitator version for yourself, or share the student version with the crew.

Only those local details change. Every observation, dose, decision and flash card stays exactly as approved.

8. After the night: feedback and attendance
  • Feedback QR shows a big QR code for the room. Crews scan it and rate the night: no sign-in needed. Feedback link copies the same form to send to anyone who left early.
  • Read what crews said under Feedback at the bottom of Run a session.
  • Record who came in Training sessions. On a session you're facilitating, tap Team list and tick each person who is there (it shows who booked). Team Leaders can also take Attendance from the session itself.
9. Ground rules
DSY Training is a training resource only. It doesn't replace the SAAS protocols or advice from the EOC Clinician. If anything on a page seems to disagree with the protocol, the protocol wins: tell the lead instructor.
  • Parts marked as written by DSY (scenarios, patient cards, EOC replies, glossary meanings) are practice material, not protocol.
  • Keep facilitator versions to facilitators. Send crews the student link.
  • Patients in the scenarios are invented. Never use real patient details in training.
