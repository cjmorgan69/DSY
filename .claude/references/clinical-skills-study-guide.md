# SAAS Clinical Skills Study Guide: page index

**SA Ambulance Service, "Clinical Skills Study Guide: Ambulance Assist, Ambulance Responder and Ambulance Officer
(Volunteer/PTS)", Version 3.2 261020, released October 2026.** © SA Ambulance Service, marked OFFICIAL. It says no part
may be reproduced or stored without written permission, so the guide itself is **not** kept in this repo (the repo
and the site are public). The owner has a scanned PDF (90 pages, images only, no text layer): ask for it when a page
needs it, read only the pages needed, and write our own short steps citing "SAAS Clinical Skills Study Guide v3.2
(Oct 2026), <skill>". First used for the anaphylaxis stations (Oxygen), the high-speed MVA stations, the falls stations and the chest pain and ACPO stations.

**Where the guide and a protocol disagree:** the vacuum mattress page (p86) says suspected spinal injury needs full
spinal precautions "including the use of a c-collar, spinal board and log roll". CPP-018-AO Spinal Injury v5.0 says
not to transport on a long board and to replace semi-rigid collars for transport where possible. The owner chose the
protocol (27 Oct 2026). The log roll page (p66) also mentions placing a spinal board; the falls stations follow CPP-018-AO
there too. The tympanic page says the thermometer is in "the side pocket of the MRx" (an older monitor).
