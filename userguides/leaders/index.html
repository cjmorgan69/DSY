1. Signing in and finding your pages

Every DSY page uses one sign-in: your ambulance ID and your password (the sign-in page also accepts an email). The same account works in Team training.

  1. Open dsytraining.com. Signed in as a leader, you see a Dashboard link at the bottom of the home page.
  2. The dashboard is at /dashboard/ (phone and tablet) and /dashboard/wide/ (computer screen). Each links to the other at the bottom.
  3. Leader pages are linked along the bottom of the dashboard, in the order you'll learn them: Team Leader training, Roster emails, Crewing gaps, Relief cover, then AR course, Widescreen version and the Retro car dash.
  4. The 👥 People button at the top of the dashboard opens the People page: adding people, teams, roles, VTLs and password resets (section 5).
Tip: add the dashboard to your phone's Home Screen. In Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

A page you're not allowed to see says "Facilitators only" or sends you to sign in. If you've been signed out, sign in again and the page you wanted opens.

Your cluster

Each Team Leader looks after one cluster: Lower Eyre, Central Eyre or West Coast. Pages open on your own cluster, and most have an All clusters chip to look wider. You can view other clusters' training but you can't change it. The lead instructor has no cluster set, which means all clusters, and can change everything.

2. The dashboard

🎬 Prefer to watch? Your dashboard is a short training module on this: a narrated video, screenshots and a quiz.

The dashboard brings the day together on one screen. On a phone it runs top to bottom in this order: Roster, Dispatch, Volunteer contribution, Training sessions, Tasks, Weather, Ask DSY, Suggestions. Lessons and microlearning are reached through Team training. It refreshes itself every 10 minutes; tap ↻ Refresh to refresh now.

Search boxUnder the heading. Type a topic (asthma, chest pain), a protocol number (CPP-050), part of a person's name or ambulance ID, or any word, and the results come up as you type: topics with a button for every part (module, walk-through, stations, slides, MDT, PCR, customiser), the SAAS protocol PDF, people (tap a name to open them on the People page), upcoming training sessions and lessons, and every page on the site you can open. A Volunteer Team Leader's team dashboard has the same box, but it finds only their own team's people. It matches words, not meaning: if nothing comes up, try a shorter word. Tap × to clear it.
Roster todayEvery team's roster for today, one row per team, one square per hour: green = 2 or more crew, yellow = 1, red = none. This hour is outlined. Use the cluster chips to filter. Tap a square to see who is on at that hour (name, level, flexibility and finish time); Back to now returns to the current hour. Team names open that team's roster. Operations overview → opens the full operations screen.
Dispatch · last 7 daysJobs paged in the last week (section 13).
Volunteer contributionA tile under Dispatch: hours on roster and jobs paged, per person.
Training sessionsThe next session, a 4-week calendar for your cluster, and a count of sessions in the next 4 weeks that have no lesson yet (tap it to fix them in Team training).
TasksYour own to-do list (section 3).
Weather · next 7 daysForecast for a town (tap a town chip to change it). A hot and windy day is flagged: check the CFS for fire danger and the BOM for warnings.
Ask DSYAsk how to do something on the DSY site (section 4).

Buttons along the top

  • Training home, 👥 People, Team training.
  • Region opens the Region page on your own cluster's teams (the lead instructor sees all clusters). Tap another cluster chip to look wider. Team training has the same Region link at the top.
  • 📅 My Google Calendar opens your calendar. Tap ⚙︎ beside it to paste your own calendar link (for example a shared calendar). It is saved to your DSY account, so it works on any device, and only you see it. Leave it empty for your normal Google Calendar.
3. Your task list (and Siri)

Each Team Leader has their own task list: other leaders can't see yours. (A relief Team Leader covering for you does see your tasks while the cover lasts: section 10.)

  1. Type the task in Add a task, pick a due date if you want one, and tap Add.
  2. Tick the box when it's done.
  3. Tap the words to change them. Tap × to delete.

Tasks that are overdue show a red date; tasks due soon show an amber date. Show done lists the last 20 finished tasks.

Siri quick add

The lead instructor can add a task by voice: "Hey Siri, DSY task", then say the task. It uses an iPhone Shortcut with a private key that adds only to that person's list. Any other leader who wants this needs their own key: ask the lead instructor to set one up.

4. Ask DSY

Ask DSY on the dashboard answers "how do I…?" questions about the DSY site, for Team Leaders only. For example:

  • "How do I reset a volunteer's password?"
  • "Why can't I edit a West Coast session?"
  • "How do I hand over to a relief TL while I'm on leave?"

It answers only from this guide and the roster guide, and links to the part it used. If the guides don't cover something, it says so. Ask the lead instructor then.

Not for clinical questions. Ask DSY doesn't answer clinical or protocol questions: use the SAAS clinical site and app. Don't type patient details or other private information into it.
  • Answers are written by AI (Claude). Check anything important on the page itself.
  • It remembers the last few questions while the page is open, so you can ask a follow-up. Start again clears it.
  • Each leader can ask up to 40 questions a day. Questions and answers are kept so the lead instructor can improve the guide.
5. People and passwords

🎬 Prefer to watch? People and passwords is a short training module on this: a narrated video, screenshots and a quiz.

Everything about people is on one page: /access/, the 👥 People button on the dashboard (also People in the Team Training header). Only people whose ambulance ID is on its list can make a DSY account.

Adding people

  1. Under Add people, paste ambulance IDs, one per line or separated by commas. You can add a name after the ID: 12345, Sam Taylor. Or choose a CSV file (ID in the first column, name in the second).
  2. Choose Add as Student or Facilitator, and the Team they belong to (or No team).
  3. Tap Add to the list. They can now sign up on the sign-in page with that ID. Sign-up asks only for the ID and a password: the account takes the name you entered here, so add names with the IDs (anyone added without one is asked for their name once, straight after signing up).

Someone who has already signed up joins the team straight away. Someone who hasn't signed up yet shows as Not signed up yet and joins the team automatically when they sign up.

Everyone

The list opens on your own cluster; tap All clusters or another cluster to look wider. People in another cluster are view only. Someone with no team shows in every cluster, so whoever brought them in can place them. Search by name, ID or team, or show only Not signed up yet, Not in a team, Facilitators and leaders, or Switched off.

  • Role: Student or Facilitator. Only the lead instructor can make someone a Team Leader or change a leader's cluster.
  • Teams: each team shows as a chip. Tap ✕ on a chip to take the person off that team (if they are its VTL, that ends too). Add to a team… puts them in another team or course. For someone not signed up yet, change the team in the box. You can only choose teams in your own cluster.
  • Remove switches the whole account off (they can no longer sign in); Add back switches it on again.

Being in a team means they see that team's sessions and appear on its Team list and attendance.

Can't see a team's members? Team lists in Team training only show people who have signed up. Crews loaded in advance with their team (for example the Central Eyre teams) are already approved: when each person signs up with their ambulance ID, DSY puts them into their team by itself. Until then they show here as Not signed up yet. Ask the crew to sign up at dsytraining.com/signin.

Volunteer Team Leaders

Each team can have one Volunteer Team Leader (VTL). Under Volunteer Team Leaders on the People page, pick the person for each team in your cluster (only people signed up in that team are listed), or No VTL to take the access away. One person can be VTL for one team only.

  • A VTL gets facilitator access (Run a session, facilitator walk-throughs) and is a roster manager for their own team.
  • They get a Team dashboard (/dashboard/team/, a button on their Team Page): today's roster and the last 7 days of dispatches for every team (the same as your dashboard), plus their own team's volunteer contribution and training, and the weather. No tasks, Ask DSY or leader links.
  • A VTL is not a Team Leader: no People page, password resets, relief cover or other teams.

You can open /dashboard/team/ yourself and pick a team to see what its VTL sees.

Relief cover

Someone covering for you (section on relief cover) can use the People page to add people (as students), put them in teams and reset passwords. They can't change roles, take people off a team, set VTLs or switch accounts off; those wait for you.

Password resets

Volunteers can't reset their own password (there's no email on file), so a leader does it:

  1. Use Reset password on the person's row on the People page (/access/).
  2. Type a temporary password (8 or more characters) and tell the person.
  3. They sign in with it and are asked to choose their own straight away.
Never post a password in a group chat or team messages. Only the lead instructor can reset another leader's password.
6. Rosters for Team Leaders

Each team has its own roster page. Everything crews and roster managers do is in the Crew Shift Roster guide. What matters for Team Leaders:

  • All 12 teams' rosters now run on DSY's own database (Lower Eyre and Central Eyre since 18 November 2026, West Coast since 19 November 2026). The Google Sheets are no longer used. Crews book with their DSY sign-in; team messages and the old change logs stayed in the sheets.
  • Team Leaders count as roster managers for their cluster's teams: you can book someone else, add a placeholder such as "LEP40" (Not on the list…), send the roster to Country Rostering and set up its weekly schedule. There's no manager password: your sign-in is enough. Part 2 of the roster guide has the manager controls.
  • Adding a placeholder before the cut-off? Tick Don't email Country Rostering under the name box so no change email goes out. Edits, cancels and crew bookings still send emails as usual.
  • Summer Bay is a test roster, not a real team: use it to try things out.
  • The dashboard's Roster today, On shift and Crewing gaps all read the team rosters, so a booking shows there within a few minutes.
7. Roster email settings

/dashboard/roster-settings/ (Roster emails at the bottom of the dashboard) holds the addresses roster emails go to, for every team's roster.

  • Addresses per cluster: trial address, area manager, Country Rostering and an optional CC. Tap Save addresses.
  • Team managers: add or remove each team's roster managers and the email their alerts go to (crews never see it).
  • Send a test email checks the emails arrive.
  • Live from date (lead instructor only): all three clusters are live. Until a cluster's date is set it is a trial: every email goes only to the trial address, marked "TRIAL", and never to Country Rostering.
  • A cluster with no Country Rostering address sends change alerts to its team managers only, so crews need to phone for changes before the cut-off. Check your cluster's addresses and team managers are filled in.
  • Recent roster emails lists the last 15 with any error.

Roster emails come from "<Team> Team Roster" (for example "Tumby Bay Team Roster"), and replies go to that team's roster managers.

8. Crewing gaps

🎬 Prefer to watch? Crewing gaps is a short training module on this: a narrated video, screenshots and a quiz.

/dashboard/coverage/ (Crewing gaps at the bottom of the dashboard) finds the hours in the next 8 days that don't meet your own rules. It only finds the gaps: you decide what to do about each one (paramedic cover or other support).

Setting your rules

  1. Tap + Add a group. A group is one or more teams checked together (for example two neighbouring towns). You can choose from your own cluster's teams.
  2. Set the minimum crew across the group (1 to 8).
  3. For each level (PARA, AO, AR, AA, REC) choose Counts, Only with a counted person in the same town, or Doesn't count.
  4. Choose the shortest gap to list (any, or 1 to 12 hours).
  5. Tap Save rules. Your rules are yours only; a relief Team Leader covering for you can read them.

A new group starts with: minimum 2; PARA, AO and AR count; AA only with one of those in the same town; REC doesn't count; gaps of 6 hours or more.

Reading the results

  • For each group: the number of gaps and hours, a bar for each day, and each gap with who was booked.
  • Gaps are counted by volunteer shift: Day 08:00–18:00 and Night 18:00–08:00. A gap that runs across several shifts is listed once for each shift it touches, marked "Part of a longer gap".
  • For groups of two or more teams, it also lists towns that have nobody counted in their own right.
  • Gaps that have already finished are left out. Today goes back to today; Copy as text copies the list to paste into a message or email.
9. Training sessions and lessons

🎬 Prefer to watch? Training nights is a short training module on this: a narrated video, screenshots and a quiz.

Training is run in Team training, /training/ (Team training on the dashboard). As a leader you see two tabs, Calendar and Lessons. People are managed on the People page (section 5).

Your cluster

The app opens on your own cluster. The Cluster chips on Calendar and People show the others, but another cluster's sessions are view only (a notice says whose they are) and their team boxes are locked. Lessons are one shared library for everyone.

Setting up your training nights

  1. On Calendar, tap Set up training nights.
  2. Choose the team, then who the nights are for: Team Leader visits (leader-led, with you as facilitator) or Team training (team-led; choose the facilitator later by opening each night).
  3. Pick the first night (the nights repeat on that weekday), how often (every week, fortnightly, every third or fourth week), the last date (it starts at the end of that school year) and the times.
  4. SA school holidays and public holidays are left out unless you untick them. The list shows every date, with the ones left out crossed through and the reason. Nights the team already has are kept as they are.
  5. Tap Create sessions.

To do your visits and the team's training on alternate weeks, set up your visits first, then set up team training starting one week later. Add an extra night with Add one session; to remove a night, open it and tap Delete session.

Sessions and courses

  • Open a session to choose its lessons (a session can have several, in order), see bookings, take Attendance and write a Reflection (your notes on how it went; members never see them).
  • Ambulance Responder course days also show on the Calendar, in purple (AR course · Day 1 and so on), with how many are booked. They are set up and booked on the AR course pages (AR course at the bottom of the dashboard): tap one to open the bookings page. The AR course filter shows only those days.
  • Delete session removes its bookings and attendance too, and can't be undone.
The dashboard counts sessions in the next 4 weeks that have no lesson. Tap the count to go straight to them.

Lessons

The Lessons tab is the library: New lesson adds one (title, summary, resources and links, files), and Use this lesson in adds it to a session. The dashboard's Lessons tile opens this tab directly (/training/#lessons).

10. Relief cover

🎬 Prefer to watch? Relief cover is a short training module on this: a narrated video, screenshots and a quiz.

Going on leave? /dashboard/relief/ (Relief cover at the bottom of the dashboard) hands your leader access to a relief Team Leader until an end date.

  1. Under Hand over, choose the Relief Team Leader.
  2. Set From and Until.
  3. Tap Start cover and confirm.
They get, until the end dateYour dashboards and your task list; training for your cluster, with editing; volunteer contribution and dispatch; the People page to add people, put them in teams and reset passwords (no role changes or removals); the Region page.
They don't getYour Google Calendar link; resetting a leader's password; making anyone a leader; handing the cover on to someone else.

The cover ends by itself on the end date. You, the relief Team Leader or the lead instructor can end it early with End now (the relief Team Leader sees End my cover). The relief Team Leader may need to refresh an open page when it starts or ends. While covering, the dashboard shows a banner and your tasks are labelled with your name.

11. Your team page

🎬 Prefer to watch? Your team page is a short training module on this: a narrated video, screenshots and a quiz.

Each team has a Team Page (/teams/) that members see when they sign in: a banner photo behind the team name, the Training schedule and Learn on your own buttons, photo slides to swipe through, About the team, and links.

  1. Open your team's page and tap Edit this page (leaders only). Or open the Region page (/region/) and tap Edit page on the team's tile.
  2. Banner photo: choose a photo to put it behind the team name. It replaces the town photo DSY put there. If the wrong part shows (a head cut off), drag the Move the photo slider until the preview looks right; it saves when you let go. Remove banner photo takes it off.
  3. Photo slides: choose one or more photos to add them. Each one can have a caption; Move up changes the order and Remove takes one out.
  4. About this team, the roster link and the links list are in the same form: tap Save team page for those. Photos save as soon as they upload.
Photos must be JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB each. Only use photos you took or have permission to use, and check that crews and patients are happy to be in them.
12. Volunteer contribution

/dashboard/contribution/ shows, for each person: rostered hours (shifts already worked, not future ones), the number of shifts, jobs paged to their team while they were rostered on, and P1 jobs. Tap a person to see their job list.

  • Choose the period: all, this month, last month, the last 30 or 7 days.
  • Use the Cluster chips, then the Team chips, to narrow it down.
  • Paramedic cover and placeholders (like "LEP40") are hidden unless you tick them.
What it can't tell you: the pager feed doesn't say who actually turned out, so a job counts for everyone rostered on at the time. Jobs later cancelled or stood down are included. Roster history starts in early November 2026 (later for Central Eyre and West Coast).
13. Dispatch

The dashboard's Dispatch · last 7 days panel shows jobs paged across the Eyre Peninsula. Full dispatch screen → opens /dispatch/.

  • Jobs run by Port Lincoln (PL) and Ceduna (CD) crews are hidden when the page opens. A button in the panel shows or hides them.
  • Jobs on the team leader call sign (CO, where the number is the person) are shown as "Team leader", not as a team.
  • The job list is copied into DSY from the pager feed by a scheduled run. If the newest job looks old, tell the lead instructor.
14. Setting up an AR course

Everything for the Ambulance Responder (AR) course starts on the AR course page at /ar/ (facilitators and leaders; on the dashboard footer as AR course). Its Run the course section links each step: build the course, students, induction, dates and roll, skill sign-off and progress, with the course days and their material below. Most steps are in Workshop bookings at /ar/course/, where each intake is a group: its course days, dates, students and attendance.

1. Create the group

  1. Tap + New group. Give it a name, for example "AR course 2027 Tumby Bay".
  2. Leave Start with the AR course days (Day 1 to 10) ticked: the days get their titles from the course completion checklist. Days 8 and 9 are consolidation days and Day 10 is the Stress Management online webinar.
  3. Set how many hours before a date students can still cancel (48 unless you change it), then tap Create group.
Tip: the box at the top of the page picks the group you are working on. Check it shows the right group before you add dates or students.

2. Add the dates

On the Dates tab, tap Build the course.

  • Tick a day and give it a date and start time (adding a date ticks the day for you).
  • + Another date for Day N runs the same day more than once, for people who can't make one date. Students book the one that suits them.
  • Consolidation days (Days 8 and 9, and any you add with + Add a consolidation day) are extra: they don't count towards finishing the course. Every other day, Day 10 included, does.
  • Each date has its own location box, so the same day can run at different stations. The location underneath fills any date left blank.
  • Set the finish time and places (for all the ticked days), then tap Add the ticked days.

To add one more date later, use + Add a date. Tap a date to change it or cancel it.

3. Add the students

On the Students tab, tap Add students from the people list. Pick their team or search by name or ID, tick each student and tap Add students. You can also paste a list (ambulance ID, first name, last name, one per line).

Each student shows whether they can sign in: Signed up, Can create account, or Not on access list (a leader adds them on the People page). Anyone not signed up creates their account at /signin/ with their ambulance ID.

Induction (before the course)

Induction can happen as soon as a new student has a SAAS ID number. Schedule it as the Induction row at the top of Build the course (it doesn't count as a course day). On the Induction tab, tap a student and tick off each item as it's done: ID card and key request, pager, uniform, AR course enrolment, vendor form, Ambulance Cover, multi-factor authentication, logins, the infection control prac, and each statement of the induction declaration (the student submits the declaration on SAAS eLearning). Ticks save as you go, and Print gives a copy for the file.

4. Students book

Students see a Your course card at the top of their training calendar, with their booked dates and a Dates to book list with Book buttons. They book one date per day, join a waiting list when a date is full, and can cancel up to the cut-off. They can also use Workshop bookings directly. You can book someone onto a date yourself from that date's page.

5. On each course day

  • On the Dates tab, tap the date, mark each student Present, Late, Left early or Absent (with a note if needed) and tap Save roll. Present and Late count as the day done.
  • The Progress tab shows who has done which days and what's still to do, and prints an attendance record.
  • Skills are signed off in saaslearn: open the Skill sign-off navigator at /ar/signoff/, pick your course (it selects the stations of the students in it, from their DSY teams) or pick stations yourself, add any other station a student needs, and tap Open on each skill. It opens that station's group in saaslearn with Edit checks on. Sign in to saaslearn first.

6. When the course is finished

On the Setup tab, tick Archived and save. Students then stop seeing the group.

One group at a time: a student in two active groups only sees the newest one, so archive a group when it finishes.
15. The retro car dash

/dashboard/car/ is just for fun: the same live numbers as an old car's instrument panel. Fuel = the share of today's team-hours still to come that have crew; speedometer = crew on shift now; tacho = jobs in the last 24 hours; temperature = today's top for your town (tap to change). Warning lights show low crew, a P1 in 24 hours, an overdue task, lessons needed, a hot and windy day coming, or a source that didn't load. Tap a gauge to open its page.

16. If something isn't working
  1. Check your reception, then tap ↻ Refresh or reload the page.
  2. Signed out or "Facilitators only"? Sign in again at /signin/ with your ambulance ID.
  3. A panel says it couldn't load: that source (a team roster, the weather or the pager feed) didn't answer. The rest of the page still works. Try again later.
  4. You can't change a session or team: it belongs to another cluster (view only).
  5. Still stuck? Ask DSY, or tell the lead instructor what you were doing and what you saw.
