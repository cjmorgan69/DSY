Part 1 Crew members

1. Getting started

Open your team's roster

Use the roster link your manager sent you, or the roster link on your Team Page. Each team has its own roster, and the team name shows at the top of the page.

Sign in

The roster uses your DSY sign-in: your ambulance ID and password, the same account as the training app. Signing in for the first time, or forgotten your password? See the Getting started guide. Only people on your team can open its roster.

Tip: add the roster to your phone's Home Screen so it opens like an app. In Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. In Chrome, tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen. On an iPhone this is also needed for reminders (section 2).

Your name and level

Your name shows at the top of the Home screen. Under it:

  • Change the name shown sets the name your team sees (for example "Sam" instead of "Samantha"). It changes on all your past and future shifts. Country Rostering still gets your full name.
  • Not you? Switch person signs out, for a shared phone or computer.
  • Choose your Clinical level: PARA, AO, AR, AA or REC (recruit / observer). It is saved to your account. A level change applies to new bookings; earlier shifts keep the level they were booked at.

Your shifts are linked to your account, not to how a name is spelt, so they follow you to any phone or computer you sign in on.

The Home screen

The roster always opens on Home. From there:

  • Roster shows who is booked (section 4).
  • Book a shift opens the booking form (section 3).
  • My bookings, My hours, Team messages and Refresh roster sit underneath.
  • Manager controls is at the bottom, for roster managers and Team Leaders only.

Tap ← Home, or use your phone's back button or swipe, to return to Home.

2. Roster reminders on your phone

The roster can send you a notification 24 hours before the roster goes to Country Rostering, listing the hours that still have no crew, so you can help fill them.

  1. iPhone: first add the roster to your Home Screen (Safari, Share → Add to Home Screen; needs iOS 16.4 or later), open it from the new icon and sign in again there. Android: Chrome works in the browser.
  2. Tap 🔔 Turn on reminders under your name and allow notifications.
  3. Tap Send a test to check it arrives.

Reminders are set per device: turn them on on each phone you use. Turn off stops them. It's one switch for the whole DSY site: the same device also gets a reminder 12 hours before each of your team's training sessions, and you can turn it on or off from your Team page, Team Training or the DSY home page too.

No banner? If a Focus (for example Sleep) is on, the notification goes quietly to Notification Centre: swipe down to see it.
3. Booking a shift
  1. On the Home screen, tap Book a shift.
  2. Choose the Start date.
  3. Choose a Start time.
  4. Choose a Finish time. Shifts can be 1 to 24 hours and can finish the next day.
  5. If you have a Flexibility restriction, choose A, B, C or Z. Otherwise leave N — No restriction. The line under the list says what the chosen code means; tap More info for a table of all the codes from the SAAS Volunteer Rosters procedure (PRO-295).
  6. Check the summary below the form.
  7. Tap Confirm shift.

You'll see "Your shift was booked successfully." Afterwards the date clears but the times stay selected, so booking the same hours on another day just needs a new date. The Book a shift screen stays open, so you can book several shifts in a row. Tap ← Home when you've finished.

The booked message also has an 📅 Add to calendar button (see section 5).

The roster checks that

  • The shift doesn't start in the past.
  • You don't already have a shift at the same time.
  • No more than three people are booked in any hour.
  • Only one recruit (REC) is booked in any hour.
A recruit takes one of the three places but is an observer, so they don't count towards crew coverage.
4. Reading the roster

Tap Roster on the Home screen. It has two views: Timeline and 7 days.

Timeline

Roster days run from 08:00 until 07:59 the next morning. A shift at 03:00 Tuesday shows under Monday.

Each hour is coloured by how many crew are on (observers not counted):

Red
No crew booked.
Yellow
One crew member booked.
Green
Two or more crew booked.

Tap a day to open or close it, or use Expand all and Collapse all.

Each hour also shows the crew's operational code (Op), worked out automatically from who is booked. An AR on as a single crew member shows Op D, or Op Z if they chose Z flexibility.

7 days

7 days opens a table of the next 7 days from today in a new window or tab. Use Previous 7 days and Next 7 days to move through the roster.

The 7-day table is read only: tapping a cell doesn't book anything. Tap Book a shift at the top of the table to go back and book.
5. Changing or cancelling a shift

Tap My bookings to see your upcoming shifts. Each one has an Edit and a Cancel button until the shift finishes. You can change or cancel your own shifts; roster managers and Team Leaders can change anyone's.

My bookings

Sat, 10 Oct at 15:00 to Sat, 10 Oct at 19:00
Sam Taylor · AO · Flex N · Op D
Edit Cancel

Adding a shift to your calendar

Tap 📅 Add to calendar on a shift in My bookings. A calendar file downloads. On an iPhone, tap View if asked, then Add to Calendar. On other phones and computers, open the downloaded file to add the shift.

The calendar entry doesn't update itself. If you change or cancel the shift, change or delete it in your calendar too.

Checking your hours

Tap My hours to see your rostered hours for the last 4 weeks up to now: the total, each week, and the shifts counted. Cancelled shifts are not counted.

Changing a shift

  1. Tap Edit.
  2. The panel opens with your current start, finish and flexibility already filled in. Change only what you need, such as an earlier finish.
  3. Check the line that shows Was … → now …. Any problem (for example, an hour that's already full) shows in red.
  4. Tap Save changes, or Keep as is to close.
  • Save changes stays greyed out until something has changed.
  • If you move the start, the finish time stays where it was where possible.
  • Once a shift has started, only the finish and flexibility can change.

Cancelling a shift

  1. Tap Cancel in My bookings.
  2. Confirm the message that appears.
  3. The shift greys out and shows Cancelling… while the roster saves it, then disappears with "Shift cancelled."
Poor reception? Wait. The roster tries up to three times and checks that the change went through, so there's no need to tap again. If it can't get any reply, it says the change may still have gone through: when you have reception, tap Refresh roster and check My bookings before trying again.
6. Changes to a roster already sent

The roster is sent to Country Rostering each week. The top of the Book a shift screen shows how far it has been sent:

Roster sent up until Mon, 19 Oct 2026, 08:00

You can still book, change and cancel shifts before the cut-off. Country Rostering just needs to know, and the roster tells you after saving whether it has emailed them or you need to phone.

What happens

  • Shift more than 24 hours away: Country Rostering is emailed automatically. You'll see "Country Rostering has been notified by email."
  • Shift within 24 hours, or your team's roster has no Country Rostering email set up, or the email couldn't be sent: a message asks you to phone Country Rostering. Please do it straight away.

Country Rostering:

1300 850 985

Your roster managers are also emailed about every change inside the sent roster.

7. Team messages

Team messages is a shared notice board for your team: availability, leave, and other roster notes. Messages from the old Google Sheet rosters were not carried over.

New messages

A red number on Team messages shows how many notes other people have posted since you last opened it on this phone or computer. It updates when the roster loads or you tap Refresh roster, and clears when you open Team messages.

Posting a message

  1. Open Team messages and type your note (up to 2,000 characters).
  2. Tap Post message.

Deleting a message

Your own messages show a Delete button on any device you're signed in on. Ask your roster manager to remove anyone else's.

Never post confidential information. No patient or clinical details, and no private contact or personal information. Everyone on the team roster can read the board.
8. If something isn't working
  1. Check you have internet or mobile reception.
  2. Tap Refresh roster.
  3. Reload the page in your browser.
  4. If it still isn't working, contact your roster manager.

Common questions

  • It asks me to sign in, or says I'm not on this team. Sign in with your ambulance ID. If you're new to DSY or not on the team yet, ask your Team Leader. They also reset forgotten passwords.
  • My bookings shows someone else's shifts, or none. Check the name at the top of Home. On a shared phone, tap Not you? Switch person and sign in as yourself.
  • Buttons are greyed out. The roster is still loading or saving. Wait a moment, or refresh.
  • Reminders don't arrive. See section 2: on an iPhone, open the roster from the Home Screen icon.

Part 2 Roster managers and Team Leaders

9. Manager controls and booking for others

A roster manager is a signed-in person set as a manager for the team. Team Leaders count as managers for their cluster's teams, and a Volunteer Team Leader for their own team. There's no manager password: your sign-in is enough. Crews don't see Manager controls.

Booking for someone else

  • Managers get a Booking for list on the booking form: Me, each team member, or Not on the list….
  • Not on the list… takes a typed name or unit for paramedics and outside crew, such as "LEP40". These have no ambulance ID and aren't counted in volunteer hours.
  • If Country Rostering already know about the shift (LEP40, for example), tick Don't email Country Rostering under the name before you book. The shift goes on the roster and in the change log, but no change email is sent. Edits, cancels and crew bookings still follow the normal rules.
  • The roster shows each person's chosen name. Managers and Team Leaders also see the full name and ambulance ID.

What's in Manager controls

At the bottom of the Home screen, tap Manager controls:

  • Send roster to Country Rostering (section 10) and Schedule set-up (section 11). Both fold out when tapped.
  • Edit sent cut-off and Reset sent status, only to correct a mistake (section 13).
  • Team hours (section 15).
  • Booking list: every active booking in a table, with a search box (name, level, flexibility or date) and a Cancel button on each. Tap Hide booking list to close it.
10. Sending the roster to Country Rostering

The roster emails Country Rostering itself: there's no printing or marking as sent. If a weekly schedule is set up (section 11), it does this on its own.

  1. Open Manager controls and tap Send roster to Country Rostering.
  2. Check the First roster day (it starts where the last roster sent finished) and the Number of days (7; 1 to 28).
  3. Tap Preview: it shows the period and who the email goes to. Open the PDF shows the roster exactly as Country Rostering will get it, with full names, ambulance IDs and levels.
  4. Tap Send now and confirm.

The roster then, in one step:

  • emails the PDF from "Team name Team Roster" (for example "Tumby Bay Team Roster"), with the team's managers copied and replies going to them;
  • moves the Roster sent up until cut-off to the end of the period (it never moves back);
  • keeps a copy of the PDF and notes the send in the change log.

The result says who it was sent to, and lists anything to check (for example a missing address). If it says Not sent, check your inbox before trying again.

Printing a backup copy

The PDF from Open the PDF can be printed. A computer gives the most reliable printout. Use these settings:

Printer / destinationSave as PDF or your printer
Paper sizeA4
LayoutLandscape
Scale100%. On a phone, lower it if the preview doesn't fit.
Background graphicsOn, so the Op colours print.
11. Weekly schedule

Schedule set-up in Manager controls sends the roster to Country Rostering by itself each week, so nobody has to remember.

  • Set Send every (day and hour), Roster starts on the next (weekday) and Number of days (7).
  • Leave Remind the team 24 hours before, with the hours that have no crew ticked to send the reminder in section 2. It shows how many people have reminders on.
  • Tap Save and switch on (later, Save changes).
  • The panel shows the next send, the period it covers, and how the last one went.
  • Pause stops it (nothing is sent by itself). Skip the next send skips one week and can be undone.
  • To send early, use Send now (section 10); the schedule carries on from the new cut-off.
  • If a send fails, it is tried again each hour (three tries) and the result shows on the panel.
12. After it's sent: changes and emails

When anyone books, changes or cancels a shift that starts before the cut-off:

  • More than 24 hours before the shift: Country Rostering is emailed automatically, with the crew member, times and change. For an edited shift, the email includes the previous times.
  • Within 24 hours, or no Country Rostering address is set up for your cluster: no email to Country Rostering. The person is asked to phone 1300 850 985.
  • Always: the team's roster managers and the area manager (where their emails are set up) get a [Roster Alert] email saying what changed and whether Country Rostering was emailed.
If an alert says the Country Rostering email failed, or the person was asked to phone, check that Country Rostering has been told.

Change log

Every booking, change, cancellation and send is kept in the roster's change log in DSY: when, what happened and who did it. The email addresses are set by Team Leaders on the Roster emails page.

13. Correcting the sent cut-off

Sending the roster moves the cut-off for you. Use these buttons in Manager controls only to correct a mistake:

  • Edit sent cut-off sets the cut-off date and hour yourself, for example when a roster was sent to Country Rostering some other way.
  • Reset sent status clears the cut-off for your team only. Changes then stop triggering Country Rostering notices until the roster is sent again. It doesn't change any bookings.
Only reset if the sent status genuinely no longer applies. While it's reset, Country Rostering isn't told about changes.
14. Removing team messages

Roster managers can remove any message in Team messages:

  1. Open Team messages.
  2. Tap Manager delete on the message.
  3. Confirm.

Remove anything with confidential or personal information as soon as you see it.

15. Team hours

Team hours in Manager controls shows every crew member's rostered hours.

  • Choose the period: the last 2, 4, 6 or 8 weeks, or Choose dates… for a from–to range.
  • One row per person: level, shifts, total hours and hours for each week, highest first.
  • Download CSV saves the table.
  • Only booked shifts count (not cancelled ones), recruits are included, and shifts crossing the start or end of the period count only the hours inside it.

Team Leaders also have Volunteer contribution on the dashboard, with hours and jobs for every team.

16. Trial rosters and the test roster

All three clusters are live: roster emails go to the real addresses. A cluster without a "live from" date is a trial: every email goes only to a trial address, marked "TRIAL", the PDF is stamped "TRIAL - NOT AN OFFICIAL ROSTER", nothing goes to Country Rostering, and nobody is asked to phone them.

Summer Bay is a test roster, not a real team, and always runs as a trial. Use it to try things out. The addresses and live dates are set on the Roster emails page (see the Team Leader guide).
