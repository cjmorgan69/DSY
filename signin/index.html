Sign in to open the training pages.

Enter your ambulance ID and choose a password of at least 8 characters. Your name comes from your instructor's list. Your ID number is only used to check you are approved and so your instructors know who you are.

Never signed in before?
Tap First time here? below, enter your ambulance ID and choose your own password. You don't need a starter password.

Your ID has to be on the list your instructor keeps. Forgotten your password? Ask your instructor to reset it. They will give you a temporary one, and you will choose a new one when you sign in. Help with signing in

One more thing: your name

Your instructor didn't add your name with your ID. Type it the way your team knows you, so they can see who you are.

Step 2: choose your own password

The password your instructor gave you was only to get you in. Choose a new one that only you know, at least 8 characters. From now on, sign in with your ambulance ID and this new password. Your phone may offer to save it: say yes.
