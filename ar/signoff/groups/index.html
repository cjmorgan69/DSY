saaslearn group finder

What this is for

saaslearn numbers its station groups separately in each course, so "Tumby Bay" has a different number in the First Aid course from the infection control one. This bookmark reads every station group and its number from a saaslearn sign-off page. Paste the result to Claude and the sign-off links get the right numbers for every team.

It only reads the page you are on and shows the list. Nothing is sent anywhere.

1. Add the bookmark (once)

On an iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Tap
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add Bookmark, and Save (it bookmarks this page for now).
  3. Open Bookmarks, tap Edit, tap the new bookmark. Change the name to DSY group finder, clear the address and paste the code. Tap Done.

On a computer: drag this to your bookmarks bar: DSY group finder

Show the code

2. Use it on each course

  1. Sign in to saaslearn, then open one of the skills below.
  2. Tap the address bar, type DSY group finder and tap the bookmark in the suggestions (or open Bookmarks and tap it).
  3. A box lists the course with every station and its number. If it says no groups were found, open the group menu on the page first and tap the bookmark again.
  4. Tap Copy and paste it to Claude. Then do the next skill.

One skill from each topic (topics that share a saaslearn course will show the same numbers):

  • Infection Prevention and Control (Hand Washing)
  • First Aid and AA Protocols (Adult CPR)
  • Manual Tasks (Postures)
  • Operational Preparedness (GRN, radio procedures and MDT)
  • DRiVE Volunteer Practical Assessments (Practical Assessment Completion)
  • Foundations of Care (Palpatory Blood Pressure)
  • Cardiac Arrest Management (i-gel)
  • SAAS Resuscitation and AA Protocols (BVM Ventilations)
  • Medical Conditions (Nebulised Medication (6+))
  • Traumatic Injuries (Femoral Traction Splint)
