How this is worked out

Hours are the active roster bookings that have already happened (future shifts are left out until they're over). Cancelled bookings don't count.

Jobs are jobs paged to the person's team while they were rostered on. The pager feed can't tell which jobs were later cancelled or stood down, or who actually turned out, so this is "paged while on shift", not "attended". Everyone rostered at that time gets the job.

Use these numbers to thank people, not to chase them.
