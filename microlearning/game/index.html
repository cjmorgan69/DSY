Protocol Chase

Eat the dots, dodge the ghosts. When a ghost catches you, answer a protocol question in time to keep your life.

  • Ghost catches you: answer correctly for immunity, and your dots score double for 10 seconds.
  • Power dot: answer correctly to power up. Then chase the ghosts, with dots scoring double while it lasts.
  • Wrong or too slow: you lose a life (ghost) or the power-up (power dot). The right answer and the reason are shown.
  • Controls: swipe anywhere on the maze, or use the arrow buttons.
