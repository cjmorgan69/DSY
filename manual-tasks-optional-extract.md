## 4. Powered Loading
Loading and unloading a stretcher will be practiced in the Practical component of the course.

- Two workers should load and unload a laden stretcher to share the load and ensure the loading arms are lined up correctly with the stretcher.
- Where possible loading and unloading should take place on flat ground but techniques to manage on uphill or downhill slopes are covered in the video and during training.
- A common cause of the stretcher failing to load, or jamming, is not having the head-end fully extended prior to loading.

## 5. Power Failure: Manual Raising and Manual Loading
Should the stretcher or the power loader lose power, there are Stryker procedures to manually load the stretcher. This is covered in this video and there are also fact cards available in the ambulance should this occur.

This does not occur very often. If it occurs on the road, remember you can always call EOC to get someone to talk you through how to undertake this task.

## 6. Stryker Push / Pull Bar Securing Knobs
Two steps (1 / 2 and 2 / 2):

**1 / 2.** The securing knobs on Stryker stretchers are not interchangeable and must be installed correctly to prevent the stretcher from moving unexpectedly when manoeuvring or transferring patients who can stand on or off the stretcher. This unplanned movement places workers and patients at risk of injury.

**2 / 2.** The handle is attached using four knobs (two short yellow knobs and two long black knobs) to four securing bolts. The short yellow securing knobs are to be secured to securing bolts closest to the head end of the stretcher, and the long black securing knobs are to be secured to securing bolts closest to the foot end of the stretcher. One way to remember this is "Blonde hair, black boots": the yellow knobs ("blonde") are installed closest to the head end, while black knobs ("boots") are closest to the foot end. Another recommendation is that when you take the bar off, re-apply the knobs loosely in their correct position to prevent the knobs being lost in transit.

When re-installing the bar make sure you do not overtighten the knobs. Tighten until they are firm enough to ensure they will not loosen and become lost. It is recommended that you develop the habit of periodically checking the knobs as they may loosen naturally due to vehicle vibrations.
