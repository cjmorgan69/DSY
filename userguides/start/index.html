1. Signing in for the first time

You sign in with your ambulance ID number and a password you choose. You don't need a starter password.

  1. Open dsytraining.com/signin.
  2. Tap First time here? Set up your sign-in.
  3. Type your ambulance ID number and a password of at least 8 characters. Your name comes from your instructor's list (if it isn't on the list, you're asked for it once, straight after).
  4. Tap Set up my sign-in. You're in.

From now on, sign in with your ambulance ID and that password. Your phone may offer to save the password: say yes.

Already on a team list? If your Team Leader has loaded you with your team, DSY puts you into that team by itself when you set up your sign-in. Your team's training sessions then show straight away.
Your ID has to be on the approved list first. If you see "That ID is not on the approved list yet", ask your Team Leader or instructor to add it, then try again.
2. Signing in after that
  1. Open any DSY page. If you're not signed in, it takes you to the sign-in page, then back to where you were.
  2. Type your ambulance ID number (not an email address) and your password.
  3. Tap Sign in.

If you signed in from the sign-in page itself, you land on your Team Page: your team's photo, the Training schedule and Learn on your own buttons, and your team's information. If you haven't been added to a team yet, the page says so and still gives you the Training schedule and Learn on your own buttons.

You stay signed in on that phone or computer until you sign out.

Tip: add DSY to your phone's Home Screen so it opens like an app. In Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen. In Chrome tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen.

Asked to choose a new password?

If an instructor reset your password, you sign in with the temporary one they gave you, then see "Step 2: choose your own password". Type a new password twice (at least 8 characters) and tap Save and continue. Use the new one from then on.

3. Forgotten password and other problems

Forgotten your password? You can't reset it yourself. Ask your Team Leader or instructor: they give you a temporary password, and you choose a new one when you sign in (section 2).

"That ID and password do not match"Check your ID number and password and try again. Still stuck? Ask for a reset.
"That ID is not on the approved list yet"Ask your Team Leader or instructor to add your ID, then try again.
"That ID already has its own password"You've set up before. Tap I already have an account and sign in, or ask for a reset.
"Enter your ambulance ID number, not an email address"Type the number only.
"This account has been switched off"Ask your instructor.
"Facilitators only"That page is for facilitators running training nights. Students use Learn on your own instead.
4. The home page

dsytraining.com asks "Where would you like to start?" and offers two doors:

  • Learn on your own (for students): pick a topic and work through it on your phone (section 5).
  • Run a session (for facilitators): everything for a training night on one page.

Below them are the Tools: the MDT simulator (practise the data terminal), the scenario customiser, Training sessions (section 6) and the Vehicle check (the crew's ambulance check and stock-to-order list). Further down are pages for other groups, such as Paramedic, Rural ED, GP clinic and the Ambulance Responder course.

DSY Training is a training resource only. It doesn't replace the SAAS protocols or advice from the EOC Clinician.
5. Learning on your own

Learn on your own lists the topics (for example chest pain or asthma). Each topic has up to four steps. Do them in order, at your own pace:

  1. Learn: a short video, the key points from the protocol, and a quiz.
  2. Practise the call: a walk-through of a job, step by step.
  3. Drill the MDT: work the data terminal from dispatch to clear.
  4. Write the PCR: fill in the patient care record for the walk-through.

A step marked "Coming" isn't built yet. Your ticks are saved on that phone only, so another phone or computer starts fresh.

6. Training nights: booking a spot

Open Training sessions from the home page (dsytraining.com/training). It shows your upcoming sessions: date, time, place, the lesson, and who's running it.

  • Tap Book a spot to let the facilitator know you're coming. It then shows Booked, with a Cancel button if your plans change.
  • If a facilitator shares a lesson with you, a Shared with you tab appears. Tap a lesson to open its links and files.

Seeing "No upcoming sessions yet"? You may not be in your team yet: ask your Team Leader to add you.

7. Your team's roster

Each team has its own roster page for booking shifts. Use the link your roster manager sent you. Most teams' rosters now use the same DSY sign-in as everything else.

Booking shifts, team messages and roster reminders are explained in the Crew Shift Roster guide.

8. Shared phones, signing out and privacy
  • On a shared phone, tablet or station computer, sign out when you finish: tap Sign out at the bottom of the home page.
  • Never share your password, and don't post it in a group chat.
  • Never type patient details or other private information into DSY pages, team messages or notes.

Stuck on something this guide doesn't cover? Ask your Team Leader.
