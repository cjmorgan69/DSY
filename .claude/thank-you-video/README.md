# Thank-you video: Eyre Peninsula ambulance volunteers

Not part of the website (folders starting with a dot are not published by GitHub Pages).
The owner's song "Show Up For You" (3:02) with photos and video clips, as a thank-you to the
volunteer Ambulance Officers of the Eyre Peninsula.

## Files
- `audio/show-up-for-you.mp3` - the song (owner's file, 14 Nov 2026).
- `lyrics.txt` - lyrics with section headings and time stamps (min:sec). `(?)` = hard to hear.
- `lyrics.srt` - the same lines as captions for the video. Both made by `make-lyrics.py`
  (edit the list in it, then `python3 make-lyrics.py`).
- `photos/`, `video/` - the owner's pictures and clips, as they are sent.

## Checks before sharing
- Consent from everyone recognisable; no patients, no patient details, no number plates
  or addresses on screen at a job.
